What these seasons tell us

These are historical observation peaks, not a forecast of fungal abundance or a safe hiking season. Weather, track access, visitor numbers and enthusiastic photographers all affect the counts. Check current DOC conditions and trip guidance before planning.

  • Fungi with photographs recorded from to ; research-grade and needs-identification observations. The month is the observation date, not upload date.
  • Records must fall within km of the sourced route after projection to NZTM2000. Bounding boxes limit downloads; points outside the actual route buffer are excluded.
  • Obscured/private locations and unknown or greater-than-1,000-metre coordinate accuracy are excluded. Published totals are unique observation IDs; the same record may be near more than one walk.
  • All fungi are included, including lichens. The data do not establish whether a mushroom was fruiting, whether a species is edible, or whether unrecorded fungi were absent.
  • Observer-days count a photographer once per recorded day, per walk and month. They help reveal concentrated recording effort but do not fully correct sampling bias. Tied peak months remain tied.
  • Walk boundaries and per-record request receipts are retained by the repeatable pipeline. Individual observation locations are not embedded in this map.
  • Whanganui Journey is a paddling Great Walk. This map is a seasonal research guide, not a navigation map.
